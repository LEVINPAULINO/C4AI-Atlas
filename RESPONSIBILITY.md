# Responsibility and legal notice

Last updated: 2026-10-27

C4AI Atlas is a research and engineering project. It organizes published work on causality for AI. It is not peer review, legal advice, medical advice, or an endorsement of any paper, author, venue, or method.

## What this site hosts

- Bibliographic metadata: title, authors, year, venue, DOI, arXiv ID.
- Our coding labels and the codebook that defines them.
- Short quotations from papers, with a section and page locator.
- Our own summaries and redrawn charts.
- Links to the original papers.

## What this site does not host

- PDFs or full text.
- Abstracts, in whole or in translation.
- Publisher figures or tables as images.
- Bulk exports of verbatim quotations.
- Reviewer names or internal review notes.

We deliberately keep the site from becoming a substitute for reading the paper.

## Attribution

Every paper page links to its DOI or arXiv record. Every quotation carries a locator and a link to the source. Charts state the data source and the version of the coding snapshot.

Quoted text remains the property of its authors and publishers. It is excluded from this project's license. Quotations are used for criticism, review, and explanation, at a length that does not replace the original.

If you believe a quotation is too long, misattributed, or otherwise problematic, contact us. We will review it and remove or shorten it when appropriate.

## Accuracy

The labels are our judgments. They are produced by a documented coding protocol and checked by automated validation plus human review. They are not facts about author intent.

P0 rows are shown with an `under adjudication` badge. They are excluded from headline totals until signed. The site should always state which data version a number comes from.

If you find a factual error, a mislabel, or a missing paper, open a data-error issue or use the feedback form. Include the paper ID, the field, the current value, the proposed value, and a source.

## Correction and takedown

We aim to respond to correction requests within seven days and to copyright concerns within three business days.

For a copyright concern, include:

- the paper and the exact quoted passage;
- the location on this site;
- the right you hold;
- the action you request.

For a label dispute, include the paper ID, the field, and the evidence. Label disputes and copyright disputes go through different review paths.

When a record is removed, we keep a tombstone entry with the date and reason category so the audit trail remains intact. We do not silently delete records.

## No endorsement

arXiv, publishers, venues, and authors have not endorsed this project. The use of a paper's metadata or a short quotation does not imply endorsement.

## 准确性

标签是我们的判断，不是作者意图的事实。标签按公开的编码协议生成，经过自动校验和人工复核。P0 论文显示 `under adjudication`，在签署前不计入 headline 汇总。站点必须标明数字对应的数据版本。

如果发现事实错误、误标或遗漏论文，请提交 data-error issue 或使用反馈表。请附论文 ID、字段、当前值、建议值和来源。
