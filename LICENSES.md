## Attribution for quoted text

Every quotation should carry a locator and a link to the DOI or arXiv record. Quotations are used for criticism, review, and explanation. They are capped and are not a substitute for the paper.

If you reuse a chart from this site, cite the data version and the project. If you reuse a quotation, cite the original paper, not this site alone.
