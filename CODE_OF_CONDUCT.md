# Code of conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

Be direct about the work. Do not attack the person. Disagreement about a label, a method, or a chart is welcome when it comes with evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing private information without consent.

Report concerns to the maintainers through the contact address on the site. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts when needed.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
